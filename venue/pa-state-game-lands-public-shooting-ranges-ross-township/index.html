PA State Game Lands Public Shooting Ranges

Public outdoor shooting range · Axe Throwing, Escape Rooms & Indoor Golf · Ross Township

Patla Rd, Ross Township, PA

Mon–Sat 8am–sunset · Sun 12pm–sunset · open year-round unless posted

$ Free to shoot; PA hunting license or ~$32/yr PGC range permit required

Free outdoor public rifle and pistol ranges run by the PA Game Commission — bring your own ammo and targets; no range officer on site. Daytime only, 8am to sunset. Requires a PA hunting/furtaker license or the ~$32/year PGC range permit (plus ID). Nearest range to Kingston is off Patla Rd, Ross Township; another serves the Scranton area off Salem Mt. Rd, Archbald.

Drive times

  • From Kingston: 20 min
  • From Scranton: 45 min
  • From Canadensis: 40 min
